habits

i like buying CDs of my favorite albums and EPs and playing them on my CD player. believe it or not, this little thing has saved my life many times during power outages. i have found out you can survive anything with a good album.

on the go and PC: i usually download my stuff via bandcamp or lucida and listen to it through my phone's music player or through wacup on my PC.

i've been honestly very sick of songs i listen getting deleted off streaming platforms or whatever, so i've been resorting to downloading music since early 2024.

though to listen to new releases i usually resort to the spotify web app with an ad blocker #idgaf
